# Asset licences and provenance

Original shared facts, validation tools and workflow contracts are MIT licensed.
Each imported document/XML/media asset retains its recorded origin and licence;
the root licence does not replace those terms.

`manifest.json` records original repository, revision/path and exact bytes.
`notices/` retains the deduplicated original required notices for
fixture-producing libraries. These notices name their authors and projects as
required; no external library source or external test implementation is included.

Some contributed fixtures have no licence at their recorded source revision;
further redistribution terms need clarification. Generated Go fixtures are
identified separately from files present in their source repository.

Public-domain text in fixtures includes Mary Shelley's Frankenstein. Other asset
rights and producer information are retained in their original fixture notices.

## ECMA-376

The specification PDFs and unchanged extracts under `specs/ecma-376/` retain
Ecma International's copyright and original notices. The repository's MIT licence
does not apply to those documents. Source editions and exact hashes are recorded
in `specs/ecma-376/index.json`. All four full PDFs match the official ECMA downloads.

Use is subject to the [Ecma text copyright policy](https://ecma-international.org/policies/by-ipr/ecma-text-copyright-policy/)
and each document's own terms. An unchanged copy of the
[policy](notices/ecma-text-copyright-policy.pdf) is included with these documents.
Preserve the full notices when redistributing.
Derived project notes are identified separately and do not replace the standard.
